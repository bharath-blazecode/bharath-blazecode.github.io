# Liquid field-map particles

- `before/`: the earlier field-map particles on approach, inside the map and during a selection.
- `after/`: the particles gathering into a small liquid drop, separating during a fast move and scattering home after a selection. `desktop-detail.png` is a closer view of the settled drop against the dark map.

Desktop images capture moments during motion; the phone view uses reduced motion. The `liquid` profile in the [archive guide](../README.md) reproduces these captures without navigating away during the selection image.

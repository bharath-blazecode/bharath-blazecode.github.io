Comfortable asking

I grew up in the UAE and moved to Brisbane for university. Starting again in a new country made me comfortable working through unfamiliar systems one piece at a time.

I’m drawn to blue-team security, identity and access, and the decisions behind security controls. Building something—and keeping useful notes when it goes wrong—is how I make sense of it.

I grew up in Sharjah and went to school in Ajman before moving to Brisbane. Away from work, I play chess and badminton, go to the gym, and train in boxing. I’m still very much a beginner on guitar.

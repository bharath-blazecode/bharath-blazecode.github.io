What I am working on

The question
Can AI help assess cyber risk?
The work
Exploring how large language models can analyse security evidence against the ASD Essentials, the Australian Signals Directorate’s framework that succeeds the Essential Eight.
The concern
Helping design and evaluate experiments to test how far AI-based assessments can be trusted.
